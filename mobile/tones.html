Mandarin syllables — all four tones

Each block is one syllable (like ma) with four tones and four different meanings. Pinyin is color-coded. Tap a Chinese character for Mandarin audio, or tap English / हिन्दी for that language (browser text-to-speech).

Tones change meaning: for example mā (媽/妈) “mother”, má (麻) “hemp”, mǎ (馬/马) “horse”, mà (罵/骂) “scold”. Below are more beginner-friendly sets in the same pattern.
